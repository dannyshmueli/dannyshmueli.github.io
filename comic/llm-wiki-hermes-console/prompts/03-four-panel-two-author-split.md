# 03 Four Panel — Why Not Let The Agent Write Everything?

Preset: four-panel
Art: minimalist
Tone: neutral
Layout: strict four-panel comic strip
Aspect: square

Create a single-page four-panel knowledge comic titled “Why the Wiki Needs Two Authors”. Minimalist black and white with teal + amber spot colors.

Panel 1 — Human-only: Danny sits under a pile of notes labeled “brain dumps”, “links”, “ideas”; calendar pages fly by; caption: “If I maintain everything by hand, it decays.”
Panel 2 — Agent-only: Hermes floods shelves with pale gray generic pages; Danny holds his nose; caption: “If the agent writes everything, voice flattens.”
Panel 3 — Split boundary: amber protected shelf labeled “human-owned” and teal workbench labeled “agent-maintained”; caption: “So the wiki gets a boundary.”
Panel 4 — Compound loop: Danny writes in amber notebook while Hermes updates teal markdown graph; a small artifact card exits and returns as stronger context; caption: “Two authors. One compounding workspace.”

Danny: modern writer-builder, dark curly hair, short beard, black hoodie, focused warm expression, amber notebook labeled “voice + judgment”.
Hermes: small winged terminal robot with teal glowing screen face, toolbelt holding markdown cards, checklist icon, link-check icon, git branch icon.
Obsidian Vault: purple-black markdown library with glowing note cards and graph-link constellations.
